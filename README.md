# Bartłomiej Dusza

### Mechatronics · Embedded Systems · Robotics · PCB Design

I develop engineering projects combining **electronics, embedded software, mechanical design and prototyping**.

Most of my projects are built around a complete development process — from concept and calculations, through CAD, PCB and firmware design, to a working physical prototype.

My main areas of interest include robotics, embedded systems, power electronics, measurement systems, low-power devices and IoT.
